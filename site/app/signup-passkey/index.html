Step 2 of 4 · Secure your wallet

Create a passkey

A passkey unlocks your Stillwater wallet with your face, fingerprint or phone screen lock. There’s nothing to write down and nothing to lose.

  • Stays on your device. Stillwater never sees your fingerprint or face.
  • Can’t be phished. It only works on Stillwater, not on look-alike sites.
  • Synced and recoverable. Backed up with your phone’s keychain, plus email recovery.
Create passkey Use a seed phrase instead

A seed phrase is 12 words you’d keep safe yourself. Choose it if you already manage your own keys.
